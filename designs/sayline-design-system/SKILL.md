Read `readme.md` first, then load `styles.css` and the compiled `_ds_bundle.js` when creating interfaces.

For visual artifacts, keep the calm editorial tone: warm paper surfaces, ink typography, Instrument Serif display copy, ember actions, moss progress, and cover-derived player atmospheres. Keep touch targets at least 44px and preserve accessible focus states.

For production work, use the tokens and component contracts instead of hardcoded colors or spacing. Keep the learner player immersive and avoid introducing a course-detail page.

If the user asks for a new design without more guidance, ask what screen or asset they need and whether it belongs to the learner app, management app, or brand.
